How this is worked out

The figure is the upper end of the 95% Wilson score interval for the share of documents with at least one wrong field, which stays honest for small samples and for zero errors:

(p + z²/2n + z·√(p(1−p)/n + z²/4n²)) / (1 + z²/n), with p the observed rate, z = 1.96 and n the documents checked

It only uses the random sample, because flagged documents are picked for being risky and would bias the rate.
